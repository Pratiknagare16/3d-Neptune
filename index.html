Neptune

Ice Giant · 8th Planet from the Sun

Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun. It has the strongest sustained winds of any planet — reaching speeds over 2,100 km/h. Its deep blue color is caused by methane in the atmosphere absorbing red light.

Neptune has 16 known moons. Triton, the largest, is one of the coldest objects in the solar system and orbits in a retrograde direction — suggesting it was captured from the Kuiper Belt.

Neptune has five principal rings — all of them very faint and composed of dust and small debris. They were confirmed by Voyager 2 in 1989.
